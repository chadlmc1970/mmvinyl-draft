Questions for Desiree

Answer these in a text, a call or in Review mode notes. Each question is listed once, even if it comes up on more than one page.

DTF and your printers

  • What are your real roll width, edge margin, gap between designs, longest sheet and preset sheet sizes in the builder? We show 22 inches only as the common DTF width.
  • Which film and powder do your printers run, and is it hot peel or cold peel? Your product page says both are available.
  • Please confirm the care steps for the film you run, and whether the care card should carry the M&M Vinyl name or stay blank for resellers.
  • Do you want to show other makers’ press settings (Stahl’s, Siser) at all, or only your own film’s sheet?
  • Does the builder mirror art for you, or should resellers mirror it themselves? Do your art-check rules differ from the general tips on the file checklist?
  • Is there a minimum sheet size or order total?
  • The two feature lists on your live gang sheet page differ a little. Which one do you want to keep?

Turnaround, hours and pickup

  • How long after the cutoff is a sheet ready to pick up?
  • What are your holiday hours (Mardi Gras week, parade days, Thanksgiving, Christmas)?
  • Do you want a “last day to order for Mardi Gras” date or a busy-season notice on the site?
  • What are the pickup rules: ID, a teammate picking up for a customer, order paid before pickup?
  • Where should people park? Is the lot shared? Is there a pickup door or window for after hours?
  • Is curbside still offered, and how do customers ask for it?

Shipping, checkout and money

  • What should we say about shipping today? Your live site says “We only offer local pick up,” and one review mentions a mailed package.
  • What FedEx options and rates should show for shipped orders, and what do you tell customers about sales tax on shipped orders (confirm with the Louisiana Department of Revenue first)?
  • Which payment methods work at live checkout today? Apple Pay and Google Pay are planned, Venmo and PayPal have been intermittent. The add-to-cart links to your live store are standard WooCommerce links and have not been tested.
  • Do you want the Ceramic Mug note (“not available for shipping”) shown here too?

Resellers

  • How do Reseller and Volume accounts differ, and where is the line between them?
  • Which resale or tax-exempt certificates do you accept?
  • Who reviews applications, and how fast do you decide?
  • Can Kixxl price gang sheets by account type, and does it support saved sheets and reorder from a past sheet? We will ask Kixxl before promising it.
  • Do you want to offer a free test strip or sample to new resellers?
  • What is your minimum order for blanks, vinyl and patches?

Teams and businesses

  • How fast do you reply to a quote request? We left it out until you decide.
  • Do you decorate finished garments for groups, or supply blanks, DTF transfers and patches for the group to press?
  • Do you take a deposit?

Creators and Learn

  • Which sublimation inks and printers, if any, do you sell or recommend?
  • Do you want us to draft a first-project guide for HTV and one for sublimation?
  • For the pricing guide, do you want sample numbers or a “typical decorator” example, and where should they come from?

Policies

  • What is your reprint policy when a custom gang sheet prints wrong or arrives damaged? The live return policy covers damaged products only.
  • Do you want a separate line for special orders?
  • Who should review the replacement for your live Terms page (dated July 31, 2021, a generic template with a blank address line)?
  • We need a short privacy page before accounts, texting and the contact form go live. Who signs off on it, and which texting service will you use?

Your story, photos and reviews

  • Please rewrite the About page in your own words: why you started, what getting the shop back meant, what a good day looks like. Is any timeline step one you do not want public, and what month did the 2025 name change happen?
  • Can you send real photos: you at the shop, the storefront and sign, the two printers running, the pickup shelf? We also need your OK to use them. The optional two-hour shop shoot in the plan would cover all four.
  • What is your Google star rating and review count? Are the two quotes from Google or Facebook (your live site says both), and may we show the first names?
