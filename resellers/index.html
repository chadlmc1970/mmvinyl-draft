How it works

Apply once. Reorder for as long as you press.

  1. Apply

    Four short fields: business name, your resale or tax-exempt certificate, contact, and what you press.

  2. Get approved

    We review your application and set your account to Reseller or Volume. Until then, Standard prices apply.

  3. Order

    Log in and your price shows. Build a gang sheet in the builder, or add blanks, vinyl, and patches.

  4. Reorder

    Next week, hit Reorder last order. Same sheets, same sizes. Done.

Questions

Straight answers

Who can get a reseller account?

Decorators and shops that press and resell. You apply with a resale or tax-exempt certificate. Anyone can still order at Standard prices without applying.

How do I see my price?

After you are approved and logged in, your price shows on products and in your cart. Prices are set by account type, so Standard, Reseller, and Volume can differ. Until approval you see Standard prices.

Do gang sheets get reseller pricing?

Gang sheets are priced by size in the builder. Reseller pricing on gang sheets is pending until we confirm the builder can do it. We will not promise it before then.

When is the same-day cutoff?

Order by 2 PM Monday to Friday or 10 AM Saturday, Baton Rouge time, and we print it the same day. We are closed Sunday.

Is same-day printing the same as same-day shipping?

No. Same-day means we print your order the same day when you order by the cutoff. Pickup at our Baton Rouge shop is the default. Shipping with FedEx, and UPS as a second option, is being set up.

Is there a minimum per design on gang sheets?

No. Gang sheets have no minimum per design, so you can mix many designs on one sheet.

Can I reorder a past order or sheet?

Your account page lists past orders with a Reorder last order button. Saved gang sheets depend on what the builder supports, and we are confirming that.

How does tax-exempt work?

Once your certificate is approved it is on file with your account, so checkout does not ask for it again.

How do I pay?

Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Venmo, and cards. Checkout continues on mandmvinyl.com for now.

What if there is a problem with my print?

Call us at (225) 478-9319 so we can look at it. Our policies cover returns and exchanges.

Where is the shop?

12232 Industriplex Blvd, Suite 19, Bldg D, Baton Rouge, LA 70809. Open Monday to Friday 8:30 AM to 5 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM.
