Gang sheet sizing basics

What size to make each design, and roughly how much sheet a pile of designs needs.

What a gang sheet is

One sheet, many designs. You place all your designs on a single sheet, we print it, and you cut the designs apart and press each one. Our gang sheet page says there is no minimum per design, so you can mix sizes freely. Build the sheet in the builder on mandmvinyl.com.

Always crop and size your artwork first. The sheet prints exactly as loaded.

Sheet lengths and other makers

  • 22 inches wide is the common DTF roll width. Common lengths other shops list are 12, 24, 36, 60, and 120 inches. (Source: Gelato's batch guide, seen through a search summary.)
  • Other widths exist, like 24 and 30 inches. A few shops build sheets up to 22 x 240 inches.
  • Makers do not always agree. Stahl's UltraColor MAX gang sheets top out at 22 x 22 on its product page, and its 2024 guide says up to 22 x 24.

Our builder's real width, longest sheet, and presets come from Kixxl. Until then, the graphic above and the 22 inch default are illustrations.

Simple tips

  • Group designs of the same size so they pack in tidy rows.
  • Keep a little space between designs so they cut apart cleanly.
  • Cut or peel off the colored stripe line on the side of the sheet before pressing (Transfer Express guide, for UltraColor MAX gang sheets).
  • Check your art with the file checklist before you load it.
