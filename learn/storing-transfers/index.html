Care and storage

Storing transfers, patches and paper

Ordering ahead is smart. Storing it right keeps last month's sheets pressing like new. Here is how to pack it, where to keep it, and how to label every bin.

  • About 3 minutes
  • DTF, patches, and paper
  • Printable bin labels
Make bin labels

Before you start

Have these ready

  • Zip-top bags or a bin with a tight lid. Big enough to hold sheets flat, without folding.
  • A few silica gel packs. They soak up moisture inside the bag.
  • Parchment paper. One sheet between each transfer.
  • A spot indoors that stays cool and dry. A closet or shelf, not the garage, the attic, or your car.

1Step 1The four rules

Every transfer, patch, and sheet of paper we sell keeps best the same way.

  • FlatNever rolled tight, folded, or stood on edge.
  • SealedIn a zip-top bag or a bin with a tight lid.
  • DryAway from damp rooms, open windows, and wet hands.
  • Cool and shadedOut of the sun and never left in a hot car.

The hot car

Transfers and patches carry heat-activated glue. A Louisiana car in the sun is the fastest way to ruin a stack. Bring them inside the day you pick them up.

2Step 2Pack a stack

Good to know

Ninja Transfers advises keeping the room under 50% humidity (retailer guidance). If your space feels damp, the silica pack and a sealed bag matter even more.

Retailer guidance

Ninja Transfers says to use DTF transfers within 3 to 12 months, and within 30 days if you cannot store them well. That is one retailer's advice for its own film, not a maker's spec for ours. For your exact stock, call (225) 478-9319 or email sales@mandmvinyl.com.

4Step 4Test-press older stock first

Before a big run from stock that has sat a while, press one and check it.

  1. Press one

    Use a spare blank or scrap of the same fabric, at the maker settings for your film. Our UltraColor MAX and Siser Easy Transfers guides have them.

  2. Check the peel

    The ink should stay on the shirt and the film should come away clean.

  3. Check the bond

    Once it cools, stretch it gently and run a fingernail along the edges. Nothing should lift.

  4. Problem? Stop there

    If ink stays on the film or edges lift, do not press the rest. Run the symptom finder, or send us a photo.

5Step 5Patches and sublimation paper

Patches
Store flat in a bag or box so sequins do not bend and the glue backing stays clean. Keep them away from heat, like DTF. Shop our patches.
Sublimation paper
Keep TexPrintR paper sealed in its pack, flat, and dry until you print. Damp paper can print blotchy.

Ask us for paper humidity

Beaver publishes a storage humidity range for its TexPrint XPHR paper, but that is a different version from the TexPrintR paper we stock. We will get you the right figure. Call (225) 478-9319 or email sales@mandmvinyl.com.
