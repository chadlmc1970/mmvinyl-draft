    3Step 3Find the center

    Most crooked prints start with a guessed center. Pick one of these and use it on every shirt.

    1. Fold and crease

      Fold the shirt in half from shoulder seam to shoulder seam. Close the press for a quick tap to set a light crease down the middle.

    2. Fold the transfer too

      Fold the transfer in half the long way, design side out, and pinch the top and bottom edges. That gives you its center.

    3. Line them up

      Open both. Lay the transfer's center marks on the shirt's crease, then measure down from the collar.

    No crease? Use a ruler

    Measure across the chest from side seam to side seam and mark half. Or lay our printed ruler with its 0 on the middle of the collar.

    4Step 4Measure down from the collar

    Measure from the bottom of the collar seam to the top of your design. That is the number the chart and the picker give you.

    Front, 3 inches
    Left chest, center chest, and full front. Four fingers laid flat under the collar is about the same.
    Back, 4 inches
    Upper back and full back sit a little lower than the front.
    Back collar, 1 to 2 inches
    Small logos and tags tuck right under the back neck.
    Oversize, 2 inches
    Big prints start higher so they do not run into the belly.

    Distances are typical, per Ninja Transfers (retailer guidance).

    Too low is the common mistake

    A full front that sits too low ends up on the stomach. When in doubt, hold the shirt up to your chest in a mirror before you press.

    5Step 5Sleeves, pockets, and hoodies

    Seams, zippers, and pockets lift the heat press off the fabric around them. The transfer then misses pressure and will not stick at the edges. The fix is to raise the spot you are pressing so it sits higher than the bumps around it.

    Sleeve
    Lay the sleeve flat with the shoulder seam off the edge of the press. Center the design on the sleeve, about 1 inch above the hem.
    Tee pocket
    Keep the print under 3 x 3 inches so it stays off the pocket seams.
    Hoodie pouch
    Up to about 6 x 4 inches. Size it for the smallest hoodie in your run so it fits them all.
    Hoodie front
    Measure down from where the hood meets the body, and keep a full front design above the pouch.

    The insert trick

    Slide something flat and firm inside, under just the print area. A mousepad cut to fit the pocket, about 4 x 5 inches to start, works well. So does parchment paper folded until it is at least half an inch thick. We do not sell pressing pillows, but these do the same job.

    Pocket sizes and the insert trick are retailer guidance from the Ninja Transfers pocket guide.

    6Step 6Youth, toddler, and onesies

    Smaller shirts need smaller designs. A design sized for an adult will wrap into the armpits on a kid's tee.

    • Youth full front: 8 inches wide. Youth left chest, sleeve, and back sizes are in the chart above.
    • Toddler full front: 6.5 inches wide.
    • Onesies: lay it flat and measure the front between the arm openings. Stay well inside the snaps and the leg openings.

    Toddler and infant spots other than the full front. We have not found a published size for toddler left chest, sleeve, or back, or for onesies. Call (225) 478-9319 or email sales@mandmvinyl.com and we will size it with you.

    7Step 7Same spot on every size

    On a team or family run, people stand side by side in photos. Prints that sit at different heights stand out.

    1. Keep the distance, not the size

      Use the same inches below the collar on every shirt, small to big. Change only the design size.

    2. Size small spots once

      Left chest, sleeve, and back collar look the same on a small and an XL. One size for all adults keeps your sheet simple.

    3. Mark your press

      Put a strip of heat resistant tape on the lower platen at the collar line. Every shirt goes on the same way.

    4. Press one, then check

      Press one of each size first, lay them out together, and fix anything before you run the rest.

    Keep transfers from moving

    Heat Transfer Application Tape holds a transfer in place while you close the press.

    8Step 8, printablePrint the placement ruler

    A one page ruler at actual size. Lay the top bar on the collar, slide the 0 to the center, and read how far down to go. It also has cut out boxes for the small spots so you can see the size on the real shirt.

    Print at 100%

    In the print window choose Letter paper and set scale to 100% or Actual size, not Fit to page. Then measure the 1 inch check square with a ruler before you use it.

    9Step 9Check before you press

    • Design size fits the smallest shirt in the run
    • Center found with a crease or a ruler, not by eye
    • Same inches below the collar on every shirt
    • Seams, zippers, and pockets kept out of the print area, or raised with an insert
    • One test shirt of each size pressed and checked
