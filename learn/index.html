At a glance

Two makers, one table

What each maker publishes for its DTF transfers. Your film may differ, so check the sheet from your supplier before you press.

Press settings published by two DTF transfer makers
SettingUltraColor MAX (Stahl's)Siser Easy Transfers
Temperature290°F (145°C). The product page also lists 325°F (163°C) for 100% cotton.290°F
Time12 to 15 seconds12 to 15 seconds
PressureMedium on the web page. The product guide says Medium/Firm.Not on the page we used
PeelHot, right away, in one smooth motionHot, right away, in one smooth motion

Sources: Stahl's UltraColor MAX page, Transfer Express Product Guide 2024, Siser Easy Transfers. Checked October 26, 2026. Makers change their pages, so check again before you rely on it. Stahl's says its settings are for its Hotronix presses and may differ on other presses.
