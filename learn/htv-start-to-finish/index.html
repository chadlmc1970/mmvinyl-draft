Step by step

HTV start to finish

Cut, weed, press, and layer heat transfer vinyl without the classic mistakes: backwards letters, gaps between colors, and edges that peel in the wash.

  • About 7 minutes
  • Siser layering rules
  • Printable layering chart
Shop EasyWeed

Before you start

Have these ready

  • Your vinyl. Siser EasyWeed is the easiest to start with. See all our HTV.
  • A cutting machine with a sharp blade and a sticky mat. We carry 12x12 and 12x24 mats and replacement blades.
  • A weeding pen or hook, and a heat press.
  • A cover sheet. Teflon or parchment, for layering and re-pressing.
  • Not sure vinyl is right? Try Which method should I use? first.

Jump to a step

  1. Mirror and cut
  2. Weed
  3. Pre-press the blank

1Step 1Mirror the design and cut

HTV is cut from the back. The vinyl sits on a clear carrier, and the carrier side goes down on the mat. That means your design must be mirrored before you cut, or every letter comes out backwards.

Some vinyl cuts the other way

Specialty films can differ. Our 3D Puff page says to cut it shiny side up. Always read the product page before you load the mat.

2Step 2Weed

Weeding is pulling away every bit of vinyl that is not part of the design. Start at a corner of the extra vinyl and peel it back low and slow. Then pick out the centers of letters like A, O, and R with a weeding pen.

  • Hard to see the cut lines? Hold the sheet up to a window or a lamp.
  • Small letters tearing? Your blade may be dull. A fresh replacement blade makes weeding much easier.
  • Leave the design on its clear carrier. The carrier is what holds it in place on the press.

3Step 3Pre-press the blank

Press the empty shirt for a few seconds. It flattens wrinkles and drives out moisture, both of which keep vinyl from sticking. Find center by folding the shirt in half and pressing a light crease.

4Step 4Press

Lay the design carrier side up, shiny side facing you, and press with the settings for your vinyl. For Siser EasyWeed:

5Step 5Peel and check

Peel the clear carrier slowly at a low angle. Watch the edges as you go.

  • Vinyl lifting with the carrier? Stop. Lay the carrier back down, press again for a few seconds, and try again.
  • Hot or cold peel? Follow the sheet that came with your vinyl. If you are not sure, ask us.
  • Done? Run your finger over every edge. Nothing should catch.

6Step 6Layer two colors without gaps

Layering is where most HTV goes wrong. Vinyl shrinks a little each time it is heated, so a bottom layer pressed again and again pulls away from the top one and leaves gaps. Siser's fix is simple: press the total time, not the time per layer.

  1. Thinnest layer first

    Start with the bottom color. Lighter, thinner vinyl goes under. Thick or textured vinyl goes on top.

  2. Tack it

    Press layer one for just 1 to 3 seconds, only until the carrier peels off. Siser's number for a heat press.

  3. Place the next layer

    Line it up, carrier on. Put a cover sheet over any vinyl that no longer has a carrier on it.

  4. Finish with one press

    Press for the rest of the total time. Two layers of EasyWeed get 10 to 15 seconds at 305°F, all in.

Glitter goes on top

Siser says glitter HTV is a top layer only. Nothing sticks well on top of glitter. Puff vinyl rises when it is pressed, so it is a top layer too.

Not sure what stacks on what? The layering chart below covers the vinyl we stock.

7Step 7Fix peeling

Peels right off the press
Lay the carrier back on, cover, and re-press. Check your pressure. Light pressure is the most common cause.
Edges lift on one side
The press may be uneven, or a seam or zipper is holding the platen up. Lift the print area with a pad or folded tee inside the shirt.
Peels after a wash
Re-press with a cover sheet. Many retailers advise waiting 24 hours before the first wash and washing inside out. That is retailer advice, not a maker spec.
Gaps between layers
Too much total heat on the bottom layer. Tack, do not fully press, until the last layer.
