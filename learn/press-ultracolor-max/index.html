Press guide

UltraColor MAX press settings

What Stahl's publishes for its UltraColor MAX DTF transfers, with the source beside every number.

Checked October 26, 2026

Your film may differ. These are the maker's numbers for this product only. Film, powder, ink, fabric, and press all change the right setting. Check the supplier sheet for the film you buy.

Steps

  1. Pre-press

    Press the empty garment for 3 to 5 seconds to flatten it and drive out moisture.

  2. Place the transfer

    Lay it print side down on the garment. On a gang sheet, cut or peel off the colored stripe line on the side of the sheet before pressing.

  3. Press

    290°F, medium pressure, 12 to 15 seconds. No cover sheet for the first press.

  4. Peel hot

    Peel the carrier right away, in one smooth motion.

The pre-press time and the stripe-line tip come from the Transfer Express product guide (page 15). The guide's note about trimming black registration marks is for UltraColor PRO gang sheets, so it is not listed here.

Where the sources disagree

Stahl's own pages do not always match each other. We list the differences instead of picking one quietly.

Temperature

The product page shows two versions: 290°F for all fabrics, or 325°F for 100% cotton and 290°F for the others. The simple answer, 290°F, is the number both versions share.

Pressure

The web page says medium. The product guide says Medium/Firm, 6 to 8 on its gauge. Gauge numbers differ by press brand, so treat 6 to 8 as a hint, not a setting.

Largest gang sheet

The product page tops out at 22 x 22 inches. The 2024 guide says up to 22 x 24. That is Stahl's own limit, not ours. See gang sheet sizing for what our builder takes.

Dye migration and washing

  • Dye migration: the product does not eliminate or prevent it. Stahl's advises the lower temperature, 290°F, for heat-sensitive fabrics like polyester.
  • Washing: machine wash cold, then tumble dry low or hang dry. Stahl's reports lab testing for 50 or more wash cycles.

Source: Stahl's UltraColor MAX page and the Transfer Express guide. Need something to hand your customers? Print the care card.

If an edge lifts

Stahl's re-press tip in its guide is for UltraColor PRO, a different product that is not DTF film, so we do not repeat it here. Check the sheet for your own film, or call us at (225) 478-9319.
