How to build a gang sheet

Our builder does the hard part for you. Upload your art, tell it how big and how many, and it packs everything onto one 22 inch sheet. Here is every step, shown in the real builder with a sample design.

  • About 5 minutes
  • 22 inch wide rolling sheet
  • Free local pickup
Start building

Before you start

Have these ready

  • Your art as a PNG with a transparent background. PDF, SVG, AI, EPS, and JPG also upload. Not sure your file will print clean? Run the Is my file ready? checklist.
  • The size you want each design on the shirt. Adult front, youth front, left chest, sleeve. The gang sheet sizing guide has a chart.
  • How many of each. Count shirts per design, plus a spare or two for test presses.
  • Any names or words you want added. You can type text right in the builder.

Jump to a step

  1. Open the builder
  2. Upload artwork
  3. Size and quantity
  4. Add text
  5. Auto fill the sheet
  6. Fine tune
  7. Check your sheet
  8. Place the order
  9. Checkout and pickup

1Step 1Open the builder

Go to our Build a Gang Sheet page and tap Start building. The builder opens right on our store. Your sheet is 22 inches wide and grows longer as you add designs, so you only pay for the length you use.

2Step 2Upload your artwork

The builder opens a window called Automated Sheet Generation. Tap Upload images and pick your files, or drag them onto the green Drop Files Here box. You can upload several designs at once. Each one gets its own card.

Good to know

Files can be up to 1 GB. A big, sharp PNG beats a small one every time, because you cannot add detail by making a small image bigger.

3Step 3Set the size and how many

Each design card shows its width and height in inches. Type the size you want it on the shirt. Leave Lock Aspect Ratio checked so the design does not stretch. Then use the plus and minus buttons by Quantity to set how many copies you need.

  1. Width and Height: type the size on the shirt. Here we changed 13.83 inches to 4.
  2. DPI tag: how sharp it will print at that size. Red bad means too big for the file. At 4 inches it reads fine.
  3. Quantity: 6 copies of this design.

Watch the DPI tag

If it turns red, make the design smaller or upload a bigger file. Aim for 300 DPI when you can. Not sure what size to use? See the gang sheet sizing guide.

4Step 4, optionalAdd text

Need a team name, a last name, or a date? Tap Text on the far left, then Add text field. Type your words, pick a font and a color, then set the width and quantity the same way you did for your designs.

  1. The Text tab.
  2. Add text field makes a new text card.
  3. Type your words here.
  4. Pick the font and color.
  5. Set the width, then the quantity below. If the height jumps, uncheck Lock Aspect Ratio, fix the height, and check it again.

Spell check

We print exactly what you type. Read every name twice before you order.

5Step 5Auto fill the sheet

Tap Auto Generate Sheet at the bottom right of the window. The builder packs everything onto the sheet as tight as it can, so you use less length. Want to place things by hand instead? Tap Manual Generate Sheet and drag each design where you want it.

The first time, a short welcome tip asks you to sign in. Signing in saves your designs so reorders are quick, but it is optional. Tap Skip to keep going as a guest.

6Step 6Fine tune margins and layout

The bar above the sheet gives you a few more tools:

Undo and Redo
Take back a change, or bring it back.
Bounding Box and Free Move
Tools for placing designs by hand. Drag any design to move it where you want it.
Margin
The space kept between designs, in inches. Keep it checked at 0.3 so designs never touch and are easy to cut apart.
Auto Mode
Leave it checked so the builder keeps your layout tidy for you.

7Step 7Check your sheet

Look over the whole sheet before you order. It prints exactly as you see it. Use the zoom buttons at the top right for a closer look.

  • Spelling on every name and word
  • Every design at the size you meant
  • The right count of each one
  • Nothing cut off at the edge
  • No DPI tags in red

Use the empty space

Gaps on the sheet are free until the length changes. Fill them with pocket logos, neck tags, or a spare of your best seller.

8Step 8Place your order

When the sheet looks right, tap Place Order at the top right. Your sheet goes into your cart on our store, ready to check out.

9Step 9Check out with local pickup

  1. Review your cart

    Your gang sheet shows as one line with its price. Tap Proceed to checkout.

  2. Add your details

    Fill in your contact details so we can reach you when it is ready.

  3. Choose Local pickup

    Free. Pick up at our shop, 12232 Industriplex Blvd, Suite 19, Bldg D, Baton Rouge. Shipping is offered at a flat rate if you need it mailed.

  4. Pay and place the order

    Card or PayPal. We email you when your sheet is ready.
