1Step 1, printableCollect sizes first

Real sizes beat any guess. Hand out the sign-up sheet at practice or send it home in folders. Each person writes a name, a size, and a number.

  1. Fill in the top

    Add your team name, who to give it back to, and the date it is due. Turn on a name on back column if the shirts get names.

  2. Print and hand out

    One sheet holds 24 people. Print more for bigger groups.

  3. Set a hard due date

    Give yourself a few days between the due date and the day you order, for late forms.

Mesh jerseys

Ink and vinyl only stick where there is fabric. On a mesh jersey, slide a cover sheet between the front and back so nothing sticks through the holes, or put just one layer on the press. That is the method Transfer Express gives in its football apparel guide.

Names on back

Use the same font and the same height on every name. Long names get narrower, not shorter, so the row lines up across the team. For upper back sizes, see the placement chart.

6Step 6Lay it out on one sheet

A whole roster fits on one gang sheet. Logos, names, and numbers all print together.

  1. Upload the logo once

    Set the quantity to your headcount. Make a second card at the youth size if you have youth shirts.

  2. Type names in the builder

    Use the Text tab, one card per name, quantity 1. Our gang sheet walkthrough shows where.

  3. Count numbers, not players

    Two kids wear 4? Set the 4 to a quantity of 2. Shoulder numbers? Add two more of each player's number.

  4. Add spares

    A spare logo and a spare of the most common numbers cover a late sign-up or a test press.

  5. Read it against the roster

    Go down the sign-up sheet line by line. We print exactly what you type.

7Step 7Work back from game day

Count backward from the day the shirts need to be worn. Give each step its own day.

  1. Game day. Shirts on the team.
  2. Hand out. A day or two before, so you can fix a wrong size.
  3. Press. Allow enough days for your headcount, plus time to redo a few.
  4. Pick up your sheet. Order by 2 PM Monday to Friday or 10 AM Saturday and we print it the same day. Closed Sunday.
  5. Blanks in hand. Some styles and sizes are ordered in, so check stock early.
  6. Sizes due. The sign-up sheet due date.
