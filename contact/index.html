Quick answers

Maybe we already answered it

When is the same-day cutoff?

Order by 2 PM Monday to Friday or 10 AM Saturday and your DTF is printed that day. We are closed Sunday. Not sure it will make it? Call us at (225) 478-9319.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8:30 AM to 5 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, closed Sunday. All times are Central. See the live hours on the Visit page.

Where do I pick up my order?

12232 Industriplex Blvd, Suite 19, Bldg D, Baton Rouge, LA 70809. You get an email when your order is ready. More on the Visit page.

Do you ship?

Pickup is the default. Shipping by FedEx, with UPS as a second option, is planned. See the shipping Preview.

For Desiree

Your live site says "We only offer local pick up." One review mentions a mailed package. Tell us exactly what to say about shipping today.

Can I get reseller pricing?

About nine in ten of our customers press their own shirts. Reseller accounts and tiers are coming. Start on the Resellers page, or call and we will talk it through.

I need a quote for a team or business.

Use the Teams and Businesses page, or pick "A quote for a team or business" in the form above.

What is your return policy?

All sales are final, with an exchange for damaged products if you tell us within two days. The full wording is on the Policies page.

Do you special-order items?

Yes, we source special orders through Company Casuals. Call us about your deadline before you count on one.
