In plain language

A gang sheet is one sheet with lots of designs on it.

Instead of ordering each design by itself, you fit them together on one sheet. You press them onto shirts one at a time, whenever you need them.

Add your designs in the builder, crop and size them, and arrange them on the sheet. The sheet prints exactly as loaded, so every background needs to be transparent first.

  • Many designs on one sheet
  • No minimum per design
  • Full color with white ink
  • Soft feel, long-lasting
  • Works on light or dark garments
  • Hot or cold peel options

Questions

DTF gang sheet FAQ

Straight answers. Where we do not know yet, we say so.

What is DTF?

DTF means Direct-to-Film. We print your designs in full color, with white ink, onto clear film. You press the transfer onto your garment with a heat press. It works on light or dark garments, and on cotton, polyester, and blends.

What is a gang sheet, and how do I build one?

A gang sheet is one sheet of film with many designs on it. You drop your designs into the gang sheet builder, crop and size them, and arrange them on the sheet. There is no minimum per design.

Open the gang sheet builder on mandmvinyl.com.

What sizes can I order, and how long can a sheet be?

For artwork sizes by garment, use the sizing guide above. Sheet sizes are chosen in the gang sheet builder.

What file should I upload?

Use a PNG with a transparent background. All backgrounds need to be transparent before loading, because the sheet prints exactly as loaded. Make the art 300 dpi at the size you want it printed. Siser and Stahls both list 300 dpi for raster artwork.

Do I mirror my artwork?

No. Upload your art the way you want it to read on the shirt.

Why do my edges or shadows look odd?

Soft edges, glows, and drop shadows are made of semi-transparent pixels. Those print with partial ink and white, so they can look rough or leave a halo. Use clean, hard edges, or flatten the effect before you upload.

What is the same-day cutoff, and when can I pick up?

Order by 2 PM Monday to Friday or 10 AM Saturday (Central time) and we print it that day. We are closed Sunday. Same-day means printed that day. Pickup is at our shop at 12232 Industriplex Blvd, Suite 19, Bldg D, Baton Rouge.

How do I press it?

Settings depend on the film. As examples, Siser lists 290°F for 12 to 15 seconds for its Easy Transfers DTF, and Stahls lists 290°F for 12 to 15 seconds for UltraColor MAX. Check the sheet that comes with your order, and see our press guides for UltraColor MAX and Siser Easy Transfers, and the typical settings by fabric.

Sources: Siser Easy Transfers, Stahls UltraColor MAX.

Machine wash cold, then tumble dry low or hang dry. That is Stahls’ guidance for UltraColor MAX (source).
