How group orders work

Four steps from idea to pickup

You do not need to know every detail. Start with what you know.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Fill out the quote form with the items, a rough count, and the date you need them.

  2. We send a quote

    We reply by email or phone with one quote for the whole order.

  3. Approve the details

    We confirm your art, sizes, colors, and counts with you before anything is made.

  4. Pick it up

    Pickup at our shop is the default. Shipping is coming soon.
