My story

How M&M Vinyl got here

M&M Vinyl started in 2013. In 2020 we went curbside. In February 2022 I sold the business.

In July 2025 I bought it back, after the buyer closed. In 2025 I also took "and More" off the name. We are M&M Vinyl.

In November 2025 I bought our first printer. On October 1, 2026 the second one was installed.

My passion is to help you create the products you want. Whatever project you're working on, I'm here to help you make it a reality.

When you work with us, you're working with the owners of the company. If you have a question about a product, you don't have to call a customer service number. You can walk through our doors.

Most of the people I work with are decorators who press their own shirts. I print, they press. I also work with small businesses, teams, creators, and people trying DTF for the first time. Same attention for all of them.
